About LifeDrop

LifeDrop is a Blood Bank Management System designed to connect blood donors with people who need blood. Our website allows users to register as donors, submit blood requests, and view blood stock information. Our goal is to make blood donation information easier to access and encourage people to help save lives through blood donation.
